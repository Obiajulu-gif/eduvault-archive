# Structured maintainer notes

Maintainer notes use explicit types, visibility scopes, lifecycle states, and
per-type retention defaults. Private notes are visible to their author and
administrators, team notes are visible to maintainers, and admin notes are
restricted to administrators. Redaction replaces content with `[REDACTED]`
without removing the note id or audit history. Retention expiry archives a note
and records an expiry event.
